## 📌 About This Repository

Welcome to my **PCB Design Repository**!

This repository is my personal collection of printed circuit board (PCB) designs, custom footprints, and related electronic design resources. ## 📐 PCB Design Workflow

My general PCB design workflow includes:

1. **Planning** — Identify the circuit requirements and select components.
2. **Schematic Design** — Create the circuit diagram and establish electrical connections.
3. **Footprint Creation** — Create or verify component footprints against datasheets.
4. **PCB Layout** — Arrange components and route the electrical connections.
5. **Design Verification** — Review dimensions, clearances, connectivity, and design-rule-check results.
6. **3D Inspection** — Inspect component placement and mechanical alignment.
7. **Manufacturing Preparation** — Generate fabrication outputs when the design is ready.

Not every project will have completed all these stages.

## ⚠️ Important Notes

* Always verify footprint dimensions and pin assignments against the manufacturer's datasheet.
* Review the schematic and PCB design before manufacturing.
* Do not assume a design has been physically tested unless its documentation explicitly confirms it.
* Manufacturing files may not be available for every project.
* Check the applicable license and attribution requirements before reusing or redistributing design files.
